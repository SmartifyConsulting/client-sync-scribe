

# Mobile Access to Invoices & Templates in Holarprac

## Current State

On mobile, the bottom navigation bar for doctors shows only 4 items: **Home, Calendar, Sessions, Settings**. There is no direct entry point to "My Holarprac" (`/practice`) from the bottom nav. Doctors must open the hamburger menu (☰) to access the sidebar, tap "My Holarprac", and then scroll through tabs to find Invoices and Templates.

The tabs on `/practice` are: Practice Details, Certificates, Pricing, **Invoices**, **Templates** — all accessible once you reach the page.
